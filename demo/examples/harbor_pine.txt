Internal policy — Warranty handling (Harbor & Pine Home Goods). Every warranty claim is reviewed by the Product Care team within three business days of submission. Approved claims receive a replacement unit from the Jeddah distribution centre; the ship date is recorded on the claim and the customer receives a tracking link the same evening. Denied claims receive a written explanation and may be appealed once within fourteen days. Accidental damage, liquid ingress and cosmetic wear are not covered. Agents must not promise a ship date before the claim shows the status Approved in the Product Care console.

Internal policy — Contact verification. Before any account detail is changed or a replacement is dispatched, the agent verifies the caller against the identity document on file and the registered mobile number. Verification outcomes are recorded as a note on the claim. The verification note never leaves the Product Care console and is not quoted back to the customer in writing.

Help centre — How long does a replacement take? Once a claim shows the status Approved, the replacement unit is picked within two business days and shipped from the Jeddah distribution centre. Standard delivery to Riyadh and Dammam takes two to four business days; other cities take up to six. You can change the delivery address until the parcel is scanned by the carrier; after that, use the tracking link to redirect it or ask the carrier to hold it at the nearest depot.

Help centre — What if my claim is denied? A denied claim can be appealed once within fourteen days of the written explanation. Attach new photos or a short video showing the fault; a different technician reviews appeals. If the appeal is denied, the item can still be repaired through our paid repair service at a fixed price listed on the product page.
