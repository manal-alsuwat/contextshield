Internal policy — Warranty handling (Harbor & Pine Home Goods). Every warranty claim is reviewed by the Product Care team within three business days of submission. Approved claims receive a replacement unit from the Jeddah distribution centre; the ship date is recorded on the claim and the customer receives a tracking link the same evening. Denied claims receive a written explanation and may be appealed once within fourteen days. Accidental damage, liquid ingress and cosmetic wear are not covered. Agents must not promise a ship date before the claim shows the status Approved in the Product Care console.

Internal policy — Contact verification. Before any account detail is changed or a replacement is dispatched, the agent verifies the caller against the identity document on file and the registered mobile number. Verification outcomes are recorded as a note on the claim. The verification note never leaves the Product Care console and is not quoted back to the customer in writing.

Internal policy — Shipping and delivery. Standard delivery inside the Kingdom takes two to four business days to Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam and up to six business days elsewhere. Orders above SAR 400 ship free; smaller orders carry a flat SAR 25 fee. Express delivery is available for orders placed before 14:00 and is dispatched the same day. Carriers are assigned automatically by destination and parcel weight; the customer cannot choose the carrier but can redirect a parcel through the tracking link once it has been scanned.

Internal policy — Returns and refunds. Customers may return unused items in their original packaging within thirty days of delivery. Returns are started from the order page, which prints a prepaid label. The warehouse inspects returned items within two business days of arrival and the refund is released after inspection: card refunds take three to seven business days to appear depending on the bank, store credit is issued immediately and carries a five percent bonus. Items returned without packaging incur a ten percent restocking fee. Personalised items are not returnable.

Internal policy — Gift cards and store credit. Store credit never expires and can be combined with promotional codes. Gift cards are delivered by e-mail within an hour of purchase and can be used across multiple orders until the balance is spent. Neither store credit nor gift cards can be converted to cash. A maximum of SAR 1,500 of store credit can be applied to a single order.

Internal policy — Loyalty programme. Members earn two points per SAR spent and fifty points per verified product review. Points convert to store credit at one hundred points per SAR. Points expire eighteen months after they are earned unless the account placed an order in the previous six months. Points are not earned on gift card purchases or on orders paid entirely with store credit.

Internal policy — Data retention. Support conversations are kept for twelve months and then anonymised. Payment details are never stored in the support system; agents see only the last four digits of a card. Customers can request a copy of their support history through the privacy portal and receive it within fifteen days. Identity documents used for verification are retained only as a reference number on the claim.

Internal policy — Escalation. Tickets marked Urgent are acknowledged within thirty minutes and assigned to a named team lead. High priority tickets have a twenty-four hour resolution target. A ticket that breaches its target is escalated automatically to the duty manager and a breach note is added to the ticket. Customers are never told about internal escalation levels; they are told the next step and the expected date.

Product manual excerpt — Meridian 24-inch kitchen compost bin. Setting up: remove the bin from the carton and wash the inner pail with warm soapy water before first use. Place the carbon filter in the housing under the lid with the printed side facing up and press until it clicks. Position the bin away from direct sunlight and heat sources; the lid seal is rated for room temperature use.

Product manual excerpt — Daily use. Line the inner pail with a compostable bag if you prefer not to wash the pail daily. Close the lid fully after each use so the filter can work; a lid that is left ajar lets odours escape and shortens the filter life. Do not place liquids, cooking oil or meat in the bin. Empty the pail every two to three days in warm weather.

Product manual excerpt — Filter replacement. The carbon filter lasts about three months with daily use. Open the lid, lift the filter housing tab and slide the old filter out. Insert the new filter printed side up until it clicks. Replacement filters are sold in packs of four; the product page lists the part number. A filter that is wet or crushed will not seal and should be replaced early.

Product manual excerpt — Cleaning and care. The inner pail and the lid are dishwasher safe on the top rack; the outer body should be wiped with a damp cloth only. Remove the filter before washing the lid. Do not use abrasive cleaners on the bamboo lid trim. Check the hinge pins once a month and tighten them by hand if the lid feels loose; do not use tools on the hinge.

Product manual excerpt — Troubleshooting. Odour escapes with the lid closed: check that the filter is seated and not older than three months. Lid does not close: check for debris on the seal and confirm the inner pail is fully inserted. Hinge feels stiff: wipe the hinge pins and leave the lid open for ten minutes. Any crack in the lid, hinge or housing should be reported through a warranty claim; do not attempt to glue the parts.

Product manual excerpt — Safety and warranty summary. Keep the bin out of reach of small children and pets. The bin carries a twenty-four month manufacturer warranty from the delivery date covering material and manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover damage from drops, liquids left in the pail, dishwasher use of the outer body, or modifications. Keep the order number for any claim.

Product manual excerpt — Meridian ceramic kettle. Fill the kettle between the minimum and maximum marks only.
